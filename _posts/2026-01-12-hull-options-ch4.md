4.1 types of rates 
    - treasury (rates on t-bills, t-bonds)
    - overnight (fed funds in US, other countries have similar system). Based on the reserve requirement for a bank leading to overnight borrowing.
    - repo rate: repo (repurchase agreement), a loan. the interest rate on the loan is the repo rate.

4.2 reference rates
LIBOR: banks submit estimates of lending rates (subject to market manipulation)

SOFR: secured overnight financing rate - volume-weighted mediuan average of the rates on overnight repo transactions
